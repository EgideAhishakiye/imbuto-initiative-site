Who we are

Rooted in Rwanda. Focused on mothers.

Imbuto Initiative is a Rwanda-based maternal health initiative working to increase postpartum survival by reducing postpartum bleeding among mothers who give birth at home.

Our mission

To increase postpartum survival across Rwanda by reducing postpartum bleeding among mothers who give birth at home.

Our tagline

More mothers surviving. More families thriving.

How we see our role

We aim to identify gaps in maternal care and work with communities, health workers, researchers, government institutions and partners to develop, implement, evaluate and improve practical, evidence-informed solutions.

Our work is developing; we will share pilot findings and measurable results as they become available.

Our priority

Reaching mothers who might otherwise be missed.

Our work is designed around the specific challenge of postpartum bleeding among mothers who give birth at home. We aim to complement existing maternal health services and coordinate with relevant health authorities and partners.

Safety comes first

Imbuto Initiative does not replace skilled birth attendance, emergency referral or clinical care. Any intervention involving medicines or postpartum care must follow national guidance, appropriate clinical oversight and coordination with relevant authorities.

Our approach

Practical solutions. Evidence-informed action.

We aim to build and improve maternal health solutions in partnership with the people and systems closest to mothers.

How we work

Start with the gap. Learn with partners. Improve the response.

We focus on understanding the barriers mothers face after home births, testing practical ways to close gaps, and learning from implementation before considering broader scale.

Listen and identify gaps

Learn from mothers, community health workers, health facilities and local stakeholders.

Co-design and implement

Work with relevant partners on feasible approaches aligned with Rwanda’s maternal health priorities.

Monitor and learn

Track implementation, review challenges and document lessons honestly.

Improve and share

Use evidence and partner feedback to refine solutions and inform future decisions.

Our commitment

Accountability before claims.

We will distinguish planned activities from completed work and share verified results when available. Our long-term ambition is to contribute to increased postpartum survival across Rwanda.
